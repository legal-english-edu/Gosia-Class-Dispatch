Lesson 2 – Vocabulary

🟠 Work and Employment
an employment contract
umowa o pracę
A clear employment contract protects both the employer and the employee.
full-time employment
praca na pełny etat
For many people, full-time employment means stability and regular income.
on a full-time basis
na pełny etat
She joined the company on a full-time basis after the probation period.
on a part-time basis
na część etatu
He works on a part-time basis because he is also studying.
to work from nine to five
pracować od dziewiątej do siedemnastej
Some people prefer working from nine to five because the hours are predictable.
to start work at a fixed time
zaczynać pracę o stałej godzinie
In my previous job, I had to start work at a fixed time every morning.
to finish work at a fixed time
kończyć pracę o stałej godzinie
Office employees often finish work at a fixed time, especially in traditional companies.
to be paid on a fixed date
otrzymywać wynagrodzenie w stałym terminie
Employees are usually paid on a fixed date every month.
remuneration
wynagrodzenie
The contract should clearly state the employee's remuneration.
wages
płaca; wynagrodzenie (za pracę godzinową lub fizyczną)
Factory workers are often paid weekly wages.
🟢 Self-Employment and Responsibility
to be self-employed
prowadzić działalność; być samozatrudnionym
She decided to be self-employed because she wanted more control over her work.
to run your own business
prowadzić własną firmę; prowadzić własną działalność
Many consultants run their own business instead of working under an employment contract.
to register a business
zarejestrować działalność gospodarczą
She decided to register a business and work with several clients.
to be your own boss
być swoim własnym szefem
It sounds attractive to be your own boss, but it also means more risk.
to take responsibility for sth
brać odpowiedzialność za coś
As a manager, you have to take responsibility for difficult decisions.
🔵 Management and Company Structure
the supervisory board
rada nadzorcza
The management board must present some decisions to the supervisory board.
the management board
zarząd
The management board is responsible for running the company.
to perform well
dobrze sobie radzić; osiągać dobre wyniki
The team performed well despite the difficult market situation.
performance
wyniki; efektywność pracy
During the meeting, we discussed the employee's performance and future objectives.
the goals we achieved
cele, które osiągnęliśmy
In the appraisal, we discussed the goals we achieved during the year.
to cooperate with one another
współpracować ze sobą
People in a strong team cooperate with one another and share information.
🟣 Appraisal, Bonus, and Recognition
an appraisal
ocena okresowa; ocena pracownicza
During an appraisal, the manager and employee discuss progress, results, and expectations.
self-appraisal
samoocena
Before the meeting, employees complete a short self-appraisal.
to recognise someone's work
docenić czyjąś pracę
A good manager should recognise employees' work, not only point out mistakes.
to recommend someone for a bonus
rekomendować kogoś do premii
I would like to recommend her for a bonus because she supported the whole team.
to nominate someone for an award
nominować kogoś do nagrody; do wyróżnienia
The manager nominated her for an award after the successful project.
to give reasons for a decision
podać powody decyzji; uzasadnić decyzję
When you recommend someone for a bonus, you should give reasons for your decision.
to justify a bonus recommendation
uzasadnić rekomendację premii
You need to justify a bonus recommendation with clear examples of the employee's work.
to justify a pay increase
uzasadnić podwyżkę
The manager had to justify the pay increase to the board.
a pay increase
podwyżka
She received a pay increase after a very successful year.
expressed as a percentage
wyrażony jako procent
The bonus was expressed as a percentage of the employee's annual salary.
🟡 Recruitment and Industry
recruitment
rekrutacja
The company has started recruitment for several new positions.
a car manufacturer
producent samochodów
She works for a large car manufacturer in the HR department.
the corporate world
świat korporacji
Many people complain about the corporate world, but it can also offer good opportunities.
🟤 Useful General Phrases
most of the time
przez większość czasu; zazwyczaj
Most of the time, I work with people from other departments.
most of my free time
większość mojego wolnego czasu
I spend most of my free time with my family.
over the past two years
przez ostatnie dwa lata
The company has changed a lot over the past two years.
to think something through
dobrze coś przemyśleć
I need more time to think this decision through before I give my final answer.
to look for ideas
szukać pomysłów
We looked for ideas before choosing the final solution.
back in those days
w tamtych czasach
Back in those days, people usually stayed with one employer for many years.
on this basis
na tej podstawie
The board reviewed the documents and made a decision on this basis.
a difficult period
trudny okres
The company is going through a difficult period, but the situation should improve soon.
🔴 Corrections and Useful Patterns
either (not too)
✅ I can't hear you either.
🚫 I can't hear you too.
I can't hear you either. Maybe there is a problem with the microphone.
Ja też cię nie słyszę. Może jest problem z mikrofonem.
a difficult period will end (not a difficult time will finish)
✅ This difficult period will not end before October.
🚫 This difficult time will not finish before October.
We hope this difficult period will end soon.
Mamy nadzieję, że ten trudny okres wkrótce się skończy.
run my own business (not business activity)
✅ I run my own business.
✅ I am self-employed.
🚫 I run my own business activity.
I decided to run my own business after several years in full-time employment.
Postanowiłam prowadzić własną działalność po kilku latach pracy na pełny etat.
how much of a pay increase
✅ how much of a pay increase
🚫 how much pay increase
I need to decide how much of a pay increase I can offer.
Muszę zdecydować, jak dużą podwyżkę mogę zaproponować.

Appraisal Season
Appraisal Season
Story 1 · Lesson 2

It is appraisal season. Anna sits down with each member of her team to discuss their performance and the goals they achieved during the year. Before each meeting, the employee completes a short self-appraisal. Anna comes prepared too, because she wants to recognise people's work, not only point out what went wrong.

Two people have performed particularly well over the past two years, each taking responsibility for work that was difficult and thankless. Anna wants to recommend both of them for a bonus, but she has to give clear reasons for her decision. She spends an afternoon thinking it through: one bonus expressed as a percentage of the salary, one pay increase to reflect a role that has grown, and a nomination for the company award. On this basis, she writes her notes for the management board and the supervisory board that will see the final decision.

After the Difficult Period
After the Difficult Period
Story 2 · Lesson 2

The difficult period is not completely over, but the company has started recruitment again. Anna reviews each new employment contract before it is signed: the remuneration, whether the role is on a full-time or part-time basis, whether the person will start and finish work at a fixed time, and the date employees are paid every month.

One candidate used to work for a large car manufacturer and is weighing two paths: a steady job in the corporate world, working from nine to five, or staying self-employed, running his own business and being his own boss. Anna does not push him either way. Back in those days, she tells him, people rarely considered registering a business of their own, but that has changed over the past two years. When the new people join, she wants them to feel part of a team that cooperates with one another, not just a list of names in the management board's report.
