Lesson 3 – Vocabulary

🟠 Holidays and Time Off

oczyścić umysł; oderwać się od problemów
A few days away from work would help me clear my mind.
to go to the mountains
jechać w góry
We usually go to the mountains when we want a more active holiday.
to go to the seaside
jechać nad morze
They are planning to go to the seaside for a long weekend.
to go to sea
wypłynąć w morze; podjąć pracę na morzu
He went to sea when he was eighteen and later became a ship's officer.
to go to the lakes
jechać nad jeziora; jechać na pojezierze
Many families go to the lakes in northern Poland during the summer.
to go to a lake
jechać nad jezioro
We could go to a lake for the weekend if the weather is good.
a couple of days
kilka dni
I need a couple of days to review all the documents.
a couple of weeks
kilka tygodni
The transition may take a couple of weeks.
🟡 Responsibilities and Processes
to have a lot of things to take care of
mieć wiele spraw, którymi trzeba się zająć
Before the meeting, I had a lot of things to take care of.
to look after something
zajmować się czymś; opiekować się czymś
She looks after several key customer accounts.
to consist of
składać się z
The management board consists of five members.
at an important stage
na ważnym etapie
The company is at an important stage in the move to a new operating model.
a transition process
proces zmian; proces przejściowy (z jednego systemu, stanu lub struktury do innego)
The team is managing a transition process from the old reporting structure to the new one.
detail-oriented
skrupulatny; zwracający uwagę na szczegóły
We need someone who is organised and detail-oriented.
to pay attention to detail
zwracać uwagę na szczegóły
In this role you have to pay attention to detail at every stage.
🟢 Documents and Formal Actions
to invoke a provision
powołać się na przepis
The company may invoke this provision if the other party fails to comply.
to invoke Article 5
powołać się na artykuł 5
The government decided to invoke Article 5 of the agreement.
to refer to a document
odnieść się do dokumentu; wskazać dokument; zajrzeć do dokumentu
Please refer to the relevant document when preparing your response.
to rely on a document
opierać się na dokumencie; powoływać się na dokument jako podstawę
The company relied on the document during the negotiations.
to verify documents
zweryfikować dokumenty; sprawdzić ich prawidłowość lub autentyczność
The finance team must verify the documents before approving the payment.
to finalise documents
dokończyć przygotowanie dokumentów; ustalić ich ostateczną wersję
We are still finalising the documents for the new agreement.
🟣 Outsourcing and Delegation
to outsource work
zlecić pracę firmie lub usługodawcy zewnętrznemu
The company plans to outsource some administrative work next year.
to delegate a task
powierzyć zadanie innej osobie, zwykle w ramach organizacji
Managers should delegate routine tasks to members of their teams.
🟠 Collective Nouns
In British English, collective nouns may be followed by a singular or plural verb. Use the singular when you see the organisation as one unit.
The company is going to outsource some of its work.
The government is considering changes to healthcare law.
Use the plural when you focus on the people in the group.
The government are considering several different proposals.
England are playing Croatia today.
🔵 Performance and Pay
to reward employees based on performance
wynagradzać pracowników na podstawie wyników
The new system will reward employees based on performance, not length of service.
based on
oparty na; na podstawie: a decision based on performance
The final decision was based on financial data.
on the basis of
na podstawie; bardziej formalne: on the basis of the available evidence
Bonuses are calculated on the basis of annual results.
year by year
z roku na rok
My responsibilities have increased year by year.
to rise slightly
nieznacznie wzrastać
My salary has risen slightly over the past year.
🟡 Discussing Problems

skarżyć się na coś; narzekać na coś

It sounds unfair.
To brzmi niesprawiedliwie.
Employees with similar responsibilities receive different salaries. It sounds unfair.
the realities of the job market
realia rynku pracy
The proposal does not reflect the realities of the job market.
🟢 Understanding and Clarification
If I understood correctly, ...
Jeśli dobrze zrozumiałam, …
If I understood correctly, the new structure will be introduced next February.
If I understand correctly, ...
Jeśli dobrze rozumiem, …

🟣 Self-Employment
to set up as a sole trader
rozpocząć działalność jako osoba prowadząca jednoosobową działalność gospodarczą
I set up as a sole trader after leaving the corporate world.
a sole trader
British English
osoba prowadząca jednoosobową działalność gospodarczą
As a sole trader, you are personally responsible for the business.
a sole proprietor
American English
osoba prowadząca jednoosobową działalność gospodarczą
A sole proprietor reports business income on their personal tax return.
to be self-employed
być osobą samozatrudnioną; prowadzić własną działalność
She has been self-employed for nearly twenty years.
to work as a freelancer
pracować jako freelancer
Before joining the company, I worked as a freelancer.
to work freelance
pracować jako freelancer; pracować na własny rachunek
She worked freelance before accepting a permanent position.
✅ I run my own business.
✅ I am self-employed.
✅ I work as a freelancer.
✅ I work freelance.
✅ I conduct my own business. (correct but more formal)
The usual modern forms are "I work as a freelancer" and "I work freelance".
🟠 Time: From and Since
from + starting point. Use from to show the starting point of a future arrangement.
✅ Under the approved timetable, the new rules are in force from February 2027.
✅ The new rules will be in force from February 2027.
Both versions are correct.
since + past starting point + present perfect. Use since for a situation that began in the past and continues now.
✅ Since February 2026, we have been responsible for the project.
🚫 Since February 2026, we are responsible for the project.
🟡 Talking About Anniversaries
This year marks 20 years since ...
W tym roku mija 20 lat od…
This year marks 20 years since I set up as a sole trader.
🔵 Company Names and Legal Forms
to incorporate a company
utworzyć i zarejestrować spółkę jako odrębny podmiot prawny
The founders decided to incorporate the company before seeking external investment.
incorporation
utworzenie i rejestracja spółki jako odrębnego podmiotu prawnego
The date of incorporation appears in the company records.
Inc. – incorporated
oznaczenie występujące w nazwach amerykańskich spółek posiadających odrębny byt prawny
The invoice must show the customer's full registered name, including Inc.
Corp. – corporation
oznaczenie Corporation, występujące w nazwach amerykańskich spółek
The customer is registered under the name ABC Corp.
Co. – company
skrót od Company; sam w sobie nie wskazuje konkretnej formy prawnej
Please make sure that the full legal name, including Co., appears on the invoice.
Ltd – private limited company
brytyjska prywatna spółka z ograniczoną odpowiedzialnością
The supplier is registered as ABC Ltd.
plc – public limited company
brytyjska spółka publiczna, której akcje mogą być oferowane publicznie
The group's parent company is a plc listed on the London Stock Exchange.
Companies House
brytyjski rejestr spółek
You can check the company's registered details at Companies House.
🟠 Private and Public in Company Law
The English word private in private limited company does not mean exactly the same as the Polish word prywatna. In British company law, a private limited company cannot offer its shares to the public, while a public limited company may offer its shares to the public.
In ordinary Polish, both types may be described as spółki prywatne because they are not state-owned. Public in public limited company does not mean państwowa.
a state-owned company
spółka państwowa; spółka kontrolowana przez państwo
The energy provider used to be state-owned, but it was later privatised.
🟡 Using Registered Company Names
In ordinary British business English, company is the usual general term. Corporation is more strongly associated with American terminology, a specific legal form, or a very large organisation. Inc. and Corp. may both identify a corporation, but they are not interchangeable in a registered company name.
Apple is a corporation in the US legal sense, but its registered name is Apple Inc., not Apple Corporation. PepsiCo is the distinctive part of the name PepsiCo, Inc.
Do not change or omit elements of a registered company name in contracts, invoices, customer records, or payment documents. Use the exact legal name, including The, Company, Co., Inc., Corp., Ltd, and plc.
The Coca-Cola Company · Apple Inc. · PepsiCo, Inc.

Anna had a lot of things to take care of before she could take a few days off, because her team is at an important stage in a transition process leading to a new operating model and she still looks after several of the largest customer accounts herself. Her responsibilities had grown year by year, and although her salary had risen slightly, her workload had increased considerably. When her manager finally agreed that she could take a couple of days off, Anna admitted that she mostly wanted to clear her mind rather than travel far, so instead of choosing to go to the mountains or go to the seaside, she decided to go to a lake two hours from the city.

Before she left, Anna made sure the work would continue, since the department consists of eight people who can cover for one another. She agreed to delegate tasks that did not require her personal involvement, asked a senior colleague to verify the documents that had to be checked that week, and confirmed that the team was in the process of finalising the new payment schedule. She also reminded everyone to refer to the relevant document rather than guess, because the board had decided to outsource some administrative work to an external provider, and the handover had to be exact. By the time she reached the lake, she could finally switch off and clear her mind.

After the break, Anna joined a call about the new pay system, which would reward employees based on performance rather than length of service. Several people had begun to complain about the proposal, arguing that colleagues with similar duties would still receive different salaries, and one of them said plainly, "It sounds unfair." Anna explained that bonuses would be calculated on the basis of annual results, but she also admitted that the model did not fully reflect the realities of the job market. When a colleague summarised the timing back to her, she checked her understanding aloud: "If I understood correctly, the new structure will be introduced next February."

Part of the work was contractual, and Anna spent the afternoon checking customer records before any invoices went out. One large client had warned that it might invoke this provision in the framework agreement if deadlines slipped, and the legal team had asked everyone to rely on the document rather than on memory. Anna was detail-oriented by nature, so she confirmed that each registered name was written exactly as it was registered with Companies House, keeping the full legal form whether the customer was an incorporated company registered as a Ltd or a plc. One supplier on the list had recently set up as a sole trader after twenty years in the corporate world, and Anna noted that since February 2026, that account has been handled directly by her team.
