Lesson 5 – Vocabulary

🟠 Hotel and Facilities
air conditioning
klimatyzacja
There was no air conditioning in the first hotel.
to choose a more luxurious hotel
wybrać bardziej luksusowy hotel
We had to choose a more luxurious hotel because the first one did not meet our needs.
to change hotels
zmienić hotel
We decided to change hotels because the first one was not comfortable.
to move to another hotel
przenieść się do innego hotelu
After the first night, we moved to another hotel in Spała.
a facility
obiekt / placówka / infrastruktura
The training centre has excellent facilities for business groups.
an office facility
obiekt biurowy
The company is planning to open a new office facility next year.
a sports facility
obiekt sportowy
The town has invested in several modern sports facilities.
a shopping facility
obiekt handlowy
The new shopping facility will include several restaurants and service points.
🟢 Meals and Rest
to have breakfast
jeść śniadanie
We usually have breakfast at 8 a.m. during business trips.
to have dinner
jeść obiad / kolację
We had dinner with our business partners after the meeting.
to have a rest / to take a rest
odpocząć
After the journey, we had a rest before the evening meeting. After the long meeting, we decided to take a rest.
Use have with meals: have breakfast, have dinner. Use have a rest or take a rest when you mean odpocząć.
🔵 Trade and Business
retail
handel detaliczny
Our company does not work directly in retail.
a retailer
sprzedawca detaliczny / detalista
A retailer sells products directly to customers.
wholesale
handel hurtowy
The company sells its products mainly through wholesale channels.
commerce
handel / działalność handlowa
Online commerce has grown very quickly in recent years.
trade
handel; także fach / zawód
The company is active in international trade. Accounting is a skilled trade that demands precision and attention to detail.
domestic trade
handel krajowy
The new rules may affect domestic trade.
international trade
handel międzynarodowy
English is very important in international trade.
foreign trade
handel zagraniczny
The report presents recent figures on foreign trade.
Do not confuse: retail = selling directly to the final customer. wholesale = selling in large quantities to shops or other businesses. commerce = commercial activity, especially buying and selling, often online. trade = buying and selling as a whole, especially between countries; also a profession or craft.
🟣 Work, Departments and Responsibilities
my company told me that...
firma powiedziała mi, że... / poinformowała mnie, że...
My company told me that we needed to change hotels.
my company said that...
firma powiedziała, że... / poinformowała, że...
My company said that the first hotel was not suitable.
🚫 My company said me that...
to be good at figures
dobrze radzić sobie z liczbami / danymi liczbowymi
People in finance usually need to be good at figures.
figures
liczby / dane liczbowe / wielkości
We need to review the overdue payment figures before the meeting.
I work as an accountant
pracuję jako księgowa / księgowy
I work as an accountant in the Accounts department.
Useful, but less natural: My position is called accountant.
Accounts
dział księgowości
She works in Accounts.
a claims-handling department
dział obsługi roszczeń / reklamacji
The claims-handling department deals with customer complaints.
to handle claims
obsługiwać roszczenia / reklamacje
Our team handles claims from business clients.
business partners
partnerzy biznesowi
We discussed the new contract with our business partners.
🟡 Money and Debt
debt
dług
The company is trying to reduce its debt this year.
a debtor
dłużnik
A debtor is a person or company that owes money.
a creditor
wierzyciel
A creditor can demand payment from the debtor.
to owe somebody money
być komuś winnym pieniądze
She owes me a large amount of money.
Say a large amount of money. Less natural here: a big amount of money.
to own something
posiadać coś
The company owns several office buildings.
Do not confuse: owe = być komuś coś winnym. own = posiadać coś.
🟤 Useful Structures
on the one hand..., on the other hand...
z jednej strony..., z drugiej strony...
On the one hand, the hotel was expensive; on the other hand, the location was excellent.
a column
kolumna
Put the customer names in the first column and the payment dates in the second.
all the exercises
wszystkie ćwiczenia
Please finish all the exercises before the next lesson.
every exercise
każde ćwiczenie
Check every exercise carefully before you send it.
all the exercises = the complete set. every exercise = each individual exercise.
🔴 Dates and Email References
today is 8 July
dziś jest 8 lipca
Today is 8 July, so the deadline is next week.
today is the eighth of July
dziś jest ósmy lipca
Today is the eighth of July, and the meeting is on the fifteenth.
today is July 8
dziś jest 8 lipca – zapis amerykański
Today is July 8, so we still have one week.
today is July eighth
dziś jest ósmy lipca – wymowa amerykańska
Today is July eighth, and the report is due soon.
on 15 July
15 lipca / w dniu 15 lipca
The meeting will take place on 15 July.
your email received 12 February
Pani/Pana e-mail otrzymany 12 lutego
Thank you for your email received 12 February.
your email of 12 February
Pani/Pana e-mail z 12 lutego
Thank you for your email of 12 February.
Possible date formats: 8 July 2026 (British / European style), July 8, 2026 (American style), 08/07/2026 (British / European numeric format – 8 July 2026).
Say: on 8 July = on the eighth of July. on July 8 = on July eighth.

A Change of Hotel
A Change of Hotel
Story 1 · Lesson 5

Anna travelled to Spała for a two-day training session with colleagues from the regional office and several business partners. The first hotel had no air conditioning, and with no other room available that night, the group decided they needed to change hotels. After checking two alternatives nearby, Anna and her colleague agreed to choose a more luxurious hotel closer to the training centre, and the group moved to another hotel just before evening. The new hotel had far better facilities: a small business centre where guests could print documents, a gym and pool, and several shops nearby.

On the first evening, Anna and her business partners had dinner together before the next day's sessions began. The schedule for the following two days was tight, so everyone agreed to have breakfast early. After the long journey, Anna decided to have a rest before dinner, and by the second evening the group had settled into a routine that left enough time to take a rest between sessions.

Figures and Follow-Ups
Figures and Follow-Ups
Story 2 · Lesson 5
Loading…
0:00
–

Anna works as an accountant in Accounts, where being good at figures matters every day. The company is active in international trade, selling through wholesale channels to retailers across the region, though a small part of the business still involves domestic trade closer to home. The report Anna was preparing that week focused mainly on foreign trade, and the company had also seen steady growth in online commerce over the past year. One afternoon, a colleague from the claims-handling department asked whether Anna's team could help handle claims from one of their long-standing business partners, since the case involved several overdue payments.

Anna opened the overdue payments file and reviewed the figures: the first column listed each debtor's name, the second the amount owed. One client owed the company a large amount of money, and Anna made a note to contact them again soon. Every creditor had the right to expect payment on time, and the company always contacted clients whose accounts were more than thirty days overdue. In her reply, Anna wrote: "Thank you for your email of 12 February." She also confirmed that the updated report would be ready on 15 July. The company owned several office buildings across the region, a reminder that owing money and owning property were two very different things in the department's daily work.
