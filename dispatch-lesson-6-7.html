Over the past few months, Anna's team had built a strong relationship with the partners in India, who were now involved in the transition. Anna had successfully transferred her knowledge to the new team, and their solid preparation meant the handover went smoothly.

Not every part of the department was so lucky. The restructuring meant the company had to make several employees redundant, and it looked likely to make part of the finance team redundant as well. Some colleagues would finish working for the company in October, and the company was trying to find jobs for some of the affected employees elsewhere in the business.

Anna hadn't spent much time on her own study this week, but she planned to make up for it at the weekend.

A Journey to Headquarters

Anna arrived in Warsaw on Monday morning and went straight to headquarters for a meeting with the finance team, running from Monday to Thursday. Nobody at reception knew the finance director's whereabouts, and the meeting had to start without him. He had been staying at a country house halfway between Warsaw and Białystok over the weekend and was expected to join later that morning.

When the director finally arrived on Tuesday, Anna told him she had already been working on the transition report since Monday. He was pleased to hear the department had already held several important meetings this week, and the two of them agreed to review the figures together before Thursday.

New Tools, New Approaches
New Tools, New Approaches
Story 4 (Lesson 7)
Loading…
0:00

Anna had a lot on her plate before the end of the month, with the transition report due and the usual month-end tasks still ahead. Even so, she tried to look on the bright side: data entry was a repetitive activity that a new tool might finally speed up. Her manager, who took a hands-on approach to the rollout, asked the team to test an AI system for drafting standard client letters.

The tool required a clear prompt before it could produce a usable draft, and Anna was excited about the possibilities it offered. Several colleagues were also interested in trying it for their own repetitive activities. The idea of relying on AI for client correspondence felt intimidating at first, especially for colleagues who had never used anything similar. After a few weeks of practice, Anna was better at writing prompts than she had been at the start, and the team agreed they needed a more sustainable way of managing the month-end workload rather than relying on overtime. A new team member was due to join Anna the following month, and she offered to show her how the system worked.
